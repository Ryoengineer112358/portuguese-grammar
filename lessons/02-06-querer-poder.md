## The forms

Both verbs are irregular, mostly in the *eu* and *você* forms. Note the open vowels /ɛ/ and /ɔ/:

| Who | querer ("to want") | poder ("can") |
|---|---|---|
| eu | **quero**<br><span class="ipa">/ˈkɛ.ɾu/</span> | **posso**<br><span class="ipa">/ˈpɔ.su/</span> |
| você · ele · ela · a gente | **quer**<br><span class="ipa">/ˈkɛ(ʁ)/</span> | **pode**<br><span class="ipa">/ˈpɔ.d͡ʒi/</span> |
| nós | **queremos**<br><span class="ipa">/keˈɾẽ.mus/</span> | **podemos**<br><span class="ipa">/poˈdẽ.mus/</span> |
| vocês · eles · elas | **querem**<br><span class="ipa">/ˈkɛ.ɾẽj̃/</span> | **podem**<br><span class="ipa">/ˈpɔ.dẽj̃/</span> |

Both are followed directly by a noun or an infinitive, with nothing in between.

## Three things to know

**1. Offering with *quer*, asking with *queria*.** *Quer* + noun or infinitive is the everyday way to offer something:

- *Quer um café?* <span class="ipa">/ˈkɛ(ʁ) ũ kaˈfɛ/</span> — Would you like a coffee?

When *you* ask for something — in a café, a shop, or from a stranger — Brazilians usually soften *quero* to *queria* /keˈɾi.ɐ/, which looks like a past tense but here simply means "I'd like":

- *Eu queria um café, por favor.* <span class="ipa">/ˈew keˈɾi.ɐ ũ kaˈfɛ puʁ faˈvoʁ/</span> — I'd like a coffee, please.

**2. *Poder*: permission, favours, and "can't right now."**

- *Posso sentar aqui?* <span class="ipa">/ˈpɔ.su sẽˈta(ʁ) aˈki/</span> — Can I sit here?
- *Você pode me ajudar?* <span class="ipa">/voˈse ˈpɔ.d͡ʒi mi a.ʒuˈda(ʁ)/</span> — Can you help me?
- *Não posso falar agora.* <span class="ipa">/ˈnɐ̃w̃ ˈpɔ.su faˈla(ʁ) aˈɡɔ.ɾɐ/</span> — I can't talk right now.

It also expresses possibility: *Pode chover.* <span class="ipa">/ˈpɔ.d͡ʒi ʃoˈve(ʁ)/</span> — "It might rain."

**3. *Pode ser* = "Sounds good."** When someone suggests a plan or offers you something, *Pode ser!* <span class="ipa">/ˈpɔ.d͡ʒi ˈse(ʁ)/</span> ("Sure, that works!") is one of the most common ways to say yes.

## Watch out

> ***Quer*, not *quere*.** The *você* form ends in *-r*.<br>
> ✗ *Ele quere sair.*<br>
> ✓ *Ele quer sair.* <span class="ipa">/ˈe.li ˈkɛ(ʁ) saˈi(ʁ)/</span> — He wants to leave.

> **"I can swim" is *sei*, not *posso*.** For a skill you've learned, use *saber* (lesson 02-08): *Eu sei nadar.* <span class="ipa">/ˈew ˈsej naˈda(ʁ)/</span> — "I can swim." *Eu posso nadar* means "I'm allowed to swim" or "I'm free to swim."

> **No preposition before the infinitive.** ✗ *Pode a me ajudar?* ✓ *Pode me ajudar?* <span class="ipa">/ˈpɔ.d͡ʒi mi a.ʒuˈda(ʁ)/</span> — Can you help me?
