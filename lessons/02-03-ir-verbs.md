## The forms

The third family of verbs ends in *-ir*. Take off *-ir* and add the ending. *Partir* /paʁˈt͡ʃi(ʁ)/ ("to leave, to depart") becomes:

| Who | Ending | partir |
|---|---|---|
| eu | -o | **parto**<br><span class="ipa">/ˈpaʁ.tu/</span> |
| você · ele · ela · a gente | -e | **parte**<br><span class="ipa">/ˈpaʁ.t͡ʃi/</span> |
| nós | -imos | **partimos**<br><span class="ipa">/paʁˈt͡ʃĩ.mus/</span> |
| vocês · eles · elas | -em | **partem**<br><span class="ipa">/ˈpaʁ.tẽj̃/</span> |

Two more: *abrir* /aˈbɾi(ʁ)/ ("to open") and *assistir* /a.sisˈt͡ʃi(ʁ)/ ("to watch"):

| Who | abrir | assistir |
|---|---|---|
| eu | **abro**<br><span class="ipa">/ˈa.bɾu/</span> | **assisto**<br><span class="ipa">/aˈsis.tu/</span> |
| você · ele · ela · a gente | **abre**<br><span class="ipa">/ˈa.bɾi/</span> | **assiste**<br><span class="ipa">/aˈsis.t͡ʃi/</span> |
| nós | **abrimos**<br><span class="ipa">/aˈbɾĩ.mus/</span> | **assistimos**<br><span class="ipa">/a.sisˈt͡ʃĩ.mus/</span> |
| vocês · eles · elas | **abrem**<br><span class="ipa">/ˈa.bɾẽj̃/</span> | **assistem**<br><span class="ipa">/aˈsis.tẽj̃/</span> |

## Three things to know

**1. Only *nós* is different from *-er*.** Compare *comer* (02-02): *como, come, comemos, comem* — and *abrir*: *abro, abre, abrimos, abrem*. Three of the four forms have the same endings; only *nós* has *-imos* instead of *-emos*. That's all you need to remember for the third family. Like *falamos*, *partimos* is also the past ("we left"); context tells you which.

**2. *Assistir* means "to watch."** It's the everyday verb for TV, films, and games. Grammar books and formal writing put *a* before the thing watched (*assistir ao jogo*), but in everyday Brazilian speech the *a* is often dropped:

- *Assisto o jogo em casa.* <span class="ipa">/aˈsis.tu u ˈʒo.ɡu ẽj̃ ˈka.zɐ/</span> — I watch the game at home. (everyday)
- *Assisto ao jogo em casa.* <span class="ipa">/aˈsis.tu aw ˈʒo.ɡu ẽj̃ ˈka.zɐ/</span> — I watch the game at home. (formal)

**3. A few *-ir* verbs change the stem in the *eu* form only.** The endings are regular, but the *eu* form has a different vowel. Two you'll need often:

- *dormir* /duʁˈmi(ʁ)/ ("to sleep") → *durmo* /ˈduʁ.mu/ ("I sleep"), but *dorme* /ˈdɔʁ.mi/ ("he/she sleeps")
- *preferir* /pɾe.feˈɾi(ʁ)/ ("to prefer") → *prefiro* /pɾeˈfi.ɾu/ ("I prefer")

## Watch out

> ***Nós* takes *-imos*, not *-emos*.**<br>
> ✗ *Nós partemos amanhã.*<br>
> ✓ *Nós partimos amanhã.* <span class="ipa">/ˈnɔ(j)s paʁˈt͡ʃĩ.mus a.mɐ̃ˈɲɐ̃/</span> — We leave tomorrow.

> **Don't regularize *durmo*.** ✗ *Eu dormo.* ✓ *Eu durmo.* <span class="ipa">/ˈew ˈduʁ.mu/</span> — I sleep. The change is only in the *eu* form: *você dorme*, *nós dormimos*.

> **Write *assistir a* in formal texts.** Dropping the *a* is common in speech, but it's considered nonstandard in formal writing.
