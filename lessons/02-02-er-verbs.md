## The forms

Verbs whose infinitive ends in *-er* work just like the *-ar* verbs of lesson 02-01, with *e* where those had *a*. Take off *-er* and add the ending. *Comer* /koˈme(ʁ)/ ("to eat") becomes:

| Who | Ending | comer |
|---|---|---|
| eu | -o | **como**<br><span class="ipa">/ˈkõ.mu/</span> |
| você · ele · ela · a gente | -e | **come**<br><span class="ipa">/ˈkõ.mi/</span> |
| nós | -emos | **comemos**<br><span class="ipa">/koˈmẽ.mus/</span> |
| vocês · eles · elas | -em | **comem**<br><span class="ipa">/ˈkõ.mẽj̃/</span> |

Two more: *beber* /beˈbe(ʁ)/ ("to drink") and *entender* /ẽ.tẽˈde(ʁ)/ ("to understand"):

| Who | beber | entender |
|---|---|---|
| eu | **bebo**<br><span class="ipa">/ˈbe.bu/</span> | **entendo**<br><span class="ipa">/ẽˈtẽ.du/</span> |
| você · ele · ela · a gente | **bebe**<br><span class="ipa">/ˈbɛ.bi/</span> | **entende**<br><span class="ipa">/ẽˈtẽ.d͡ʒi/</span> |
| nós | **bebemos**<br><span class="ipa">/beˈbẽ.mus/</span> | **entendemos**<br><span class="ipa">/ẽ.tẽˈdẽ.mus/</span> |
| vocês · eles · elas | **bebem**<br><span class="ipa">/ˈbɛ.bẽj̃/</span> | **entendem**<br><span class="ipa">/ẽˈtẽ.dẽj̃/</span> |

The final *-e* is pronounced /i/, so *come* ends in /mi/ and *entende* in /d͡ʒi/.

## Three things to know

**1. Only the *eu* form is the same as in *-ar* verbs.** Put the two patterns side by side: *falo* / *como*, but *fala* / *come*, *falamos* / *comemos*, *falam* / *comem*. The vowel of the ending tells you which family a verb belongs to, so don't carry *-amos* over to an *-er* verb.

**2. *A gente* takes the *-e* form**, just as it took *-a* with *-ar* verbs:

- *A gente bebe muito café.* <span class="ipa">/a ˈʒẽ.t͡ʃi ˈbɛ.bi ˈmũj̃.tu kaˈfɛ/</span> — We drink a lot of coffee.

**3. Many everyday verbs follow this pattern** — *aprender* /a.pɾẽˈde(ʁ)/ ("to learn"), *escrever* /is.kɾeˈve(ʁ)/ ("to write"), *vender* /vẽˈde(ʁ)/ ("to sell"), *correr* /koˈʁe(ʁ)/ ("to run"). But several of the most common *-er* verbs are irregular — *ser* (lesson 01-02), *ter*, *fazer*, *querer*, *poder* — and get their own lessons in this chapter.

## Watch out

> **Keep the endings apart.**<br>
> ✗ *Nós comamos.* ✗ *A gente comemos.*<br>
> ✓ *Nós comemos.* <span class="ipa">/ˈnɔ(j)s koˈmẽ.mus/</span> — We eat.<br>
> ✓ *A gente come.* <span class="ipa">/a ˈʒẽ.t͡ʃi ˈkõ.mi/</span> — We eat.

> **The stressed *e* or *o* opens — except in the *eu* form.** In many *-er* verbs, *eu* has a closed vowel and the other stem-stressed forms an open one: *bebo* /ˈbe.bu/ but *bebe* /ˈbɛ.bi/; *corro* /ˈko.ʁu/ but *corre* /ˈkɔ.ʁi/. Before *m* or *n* the vowel stays closed throughout: *como* /ˈkõ.mu/, *come* /ˈkõ.mi/.
